# Jurerê Tradicional: beaches, getting here and when to come

Both apartments are at Rua Luiz Rampa, 70, in Jurerê Tradicional, on the north side of Santa Catarina Island in Florianópolis. The sand is 50 m away, at the end of the street. This page covers what helps you plan: the beaches, how to get here and what each month is like.

## The beaches

### Jurerê Tradicional, at the end of the street

From the building's gate to the sand is a 50 m walk.

Each apartment comes with free parking, so the car can stay put for your whole stay. You walk to the beach, carrying the chairs and umbrella we leave in the apartment.

### Jurerê Tradicional vs. Jurerê Internacional

Jurerê is one beach with two neighbouring sides along the same shore. **The apartments are on the Tradicional side.**

Jurerê Internacional is a planned neighbourhood, open to the public, which according to its developer runs its own water, sewage, security and maintenance. It is where the beach clubs and the Jurerê Open Shopping centre are. In 2009, Jurerê Internacional became the first beach in Brazil to earn a Blue Flag.

In short: if you are coming for the beach club parties, they happen in Internacional; daily life at the apartment is in Tradicional.

### Praia do Forte

Praia do Forte is where you reach the Fortaleza de São José da Ponta Grossa, the only one of the three forts run by the federal university (UFSC) that you can get to by land (see "Practical tips").

## When to come, month by month

The southern hemisphere seasons apply: summer is December to February. The sea stays above 24 °C (75 °F) from December to April and drops to about 19 °C (66 °F) in August and September. High season runs from December to February, with January and February the busiest. The minimum stay is 4 nights all year, New Year's and Carnival included.

|---|---|---|
| January | ~26 °C / 78 °F | High season, busy beach. |
| February | ~26 °C / 78 °F | High season and Carnival. |
| March | ~25.5 °C / 78 °F | Air averages 24.4 °C (76 °F), highs near 29 °C (84 °F); one of the rainier months, around 180 mm. |
| April | ~24.5 °C / 76 °F | Sea still warm, outside high season. |

| November | ~22.4 °C / 72 °F | Sea close to summer levels. |

Sources: water temperature from temperaturadomar.pt (estimated averages, not measurements); March and December air and rain from INMET 1991–2020 normals.

## Practical tips

- **Minimum stay:** 4 nights, all year.
- **Car:** free parking at the building. The beach is a walk.
- **In the sea:** swim near a lifeguard post and stay out where there is a red flag. With children, ask at the post for an ID wristband. That is the advice of the Santa Catarina fire and rescue service (CBMSC).
- **Fortaleza de São José da Ponta Grossa:** reached via Servidão da Carioca, on Praia do Forte. In 2026, entry is free on the last Sunday of each month, through November.
- **Payment and cancellation:** we agree on them over WhatsApp.
