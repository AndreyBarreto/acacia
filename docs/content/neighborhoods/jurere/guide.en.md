# Jurerê Tradicional: beaches, getting here and when to come

Both apartments are at Rua Acácio Melo, 70, in Jurerê Tradicional, on the north side of Santa Catarina Island in Florianópolis. The sand is 50 m away, at the end of the street. This page covers what helps you plan: the beaches, the trip from the airport, where to eat and what each month is like.

## The beaches

### Jurerê Tradicional, at the end of the street

From the building's gate to the sand is a 50 m walk. ### Jurerê Tradicional vs. Jurerê Internacional

Jurerê is one beach with two neighbouring sides along the same shore. **The apartments are on the Tradicional side.** <!-- TODO confirmar: beach length (~3 km in the developer's source) and whether it covers the whole shore or only Internacional. -->

Tradicional is the older, residential side, with small local shops. <!-- TODO confirmar: described only in blogs and commercial guides (Viagens e Caminhos, MySide, Rocks Investments); find a better source or have the family validate. -->

Jurerê Internacional is a planned neighbourhood, open to the public, which according to its developer runs its own water, sewage, security and maintenance. It is where the beach clubs and the Jurerê Open Shopping centre are. In summer that side of the beach gets crowded, with music from the beach clubs, and parking near the sand tends to fill up. <!-- TODO confirmar: parking full in high season (single source, Guia Viagens Brasil). --> In 2009, Jurerê Internacional became the first beach in Brazil to earn a Blue Flag.

In short: if you are coming for the beach club parties, they happen in Internacional; daily life at the apartment is in Tradicional.

## When to come, month by month

The southern hemisphere seasons apply: summer is December to February. The sea stays above 24 °C (75 °F) from December to April and drops to about 19 °C (66 °F) in August and September. High season runs from December to February, with January and February the busiest. The minimum stay is 4 nights all year, New Year's and Carnival included.

## Practical tips

- **Minimum stay:** 4 nights, all year.
- **Car:** free parking at the building. The beach is a walk.
- **In the sea:** swim near a lifeguard post and stay out where there is a red flag. With children, ask at the post for an ID wristband. That is the advice of the Santa Catarina fire and rescue service (CBMSC). <!-- TODO confirmar: whether there is a post on the Tradicional stretch, and in which months and hours. -->
- **Fortaleza de São José da Ponta Grossa:** reached via Servidão da Carioca, on Praia do Forte. In 2026, entry is free on the last Sunday of each month, through November. <!-- TODO confirmar: regular hours and price on the UFSC website (old sources say 8 am–5 pm and R$ 8). -->
- **Nearby, by car:** Canasvieiras is about 2 km away, the Cachoeira do Bom Jesus about 4.5 km and the Ratones River mangrove about 5 km. <!-- TODO confirmar: Booking distances (straight line); measure on Google Maps. -->
- **Payment and cancellation:** we agree on them over WhatsApp.
